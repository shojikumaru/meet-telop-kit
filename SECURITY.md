# Security policy

## Reporting a problem

Report security problems privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository:

<https://github.com/shojikumaru/meet-telop-kit/security/advisories/new>

Only the maintainers can read these reports. Do not open a public issue for a security problem.

## What counts

- **Leaks of recordings or links** — the kit sends a recording, a rendered video or a Drive share link anywhere other than the user's own copy
- **Delivery to the wrong repository** — a video lands in a repository other than the user's own copy
- **Guard bypass** — the kit's checks can be tricked into skipping a safety step (for example the network limits or the delivery checks)

## What to include

- what you did and what happened, step by step
- the kit version or commit you used

Never attach recordings, rendered videos, share links or real people's names. Describe them with placeholders instead.
